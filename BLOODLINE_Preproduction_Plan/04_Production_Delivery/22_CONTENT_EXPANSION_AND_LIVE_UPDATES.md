# Content Expansion and Live Updates

## Purpose

Plan future growth without making launch depend on an unproven live-service operation. New fighters, equipment, arenas, challenges, story chapters, and online features should reuse the established data and asset pipelines.

## Expansion categories

- Fighters with new archetypes or a new expression of an existing archetype.
- Arenas that add visual variety while preserving collision/camera rules.
- Signature weapons or alternate movesets, each with a full test budget.
- Cosmetic skins and gear, separated from combat balance where possible.
- Limited challenges or tournaments with fixed, transparent reward rules.
- Story chapters and boss encounters after campaign tooling is proven.
- Online events/ranked seasons only after secure services, moderation, support, and operational capacity exist.

## Fighter release gate

No fighter ships without approved concept, model, textures, rig, animation, move data, unique ability, UI art, audio/VFX, unlock/reward configuration, licence evidence, compatibility tests, performance test, and balance review. A new fighter must have a reason to exist beyond a recolour.

## Versioning and compatibility

Content definitions and save data need versioning. If an update modifies move IDs or unlock paths, include a migration plan. Avoid removing owned items without a player-facing policy. Keep older save samples in regression tests.

## Balance updates

Record why a change is proposed, its impact on matchups and progression, and how it will be tested. Prefer small traceable adjustments over wholesale undocumented retuning. Maintain patch notes written in player-understandable language.

## Operational readiness

Live updates require a release owner, build and rollback plan, crash/error monitoring, support process, communication channel, privacy handling, and a reliable way to diagnose version-specific defects. Do not promise a release cadence before the capacity to sustain it exists.

## Monetisation policy

Monetisation is not approved by the current plan. If considered later, define player value, pricing, refund/support procedures, platform compliance, regional requirements, and protections for minors where relevant. Avoid loot-box mechanics unless a separate legal and platform-policy review confirms the design is permitted and the odds/disclosures are handled properly. Competitive fairness must remain a priority.
